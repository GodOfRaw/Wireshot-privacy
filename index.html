During the beta we ask testers, above all those with cameras we do not own (Nikon and Sony in particular, but also Canon, Fujifilm, Olympus, Pentax and others), to create a Compatibility Report and to send us the Camera Log. This is how we learn what each camera reports and which cameras to add or fix. Both are made on your device. Nothing is sent automatically, and nothing is sent unless you press Share and choose where it goes (for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files). You can read them first and you can refuse; the app works the same either way. Where a report goes after you press Share depends on the service you choose, under that service's own terms. What a Compatibility Report contains: • The camera's make, model and firmware version, as the camera reports them. Some cameras also report a name that their owner can set: if yours does, check it before you send. • What the camera says it can do: the lists of commands, events, properties and picture formats it supports (technical codes), and a short automatic assessment. • Counts of what happened while it was connected: files on the card, new shots announced, camera events by type. • The app version, the iPadOS or iOS version, the device model (for example "iPad"), and the date and time it was made. What the Camera Log contains: • A time-stamped list of the steps between the app and the camera: connecting, opening and closing the session, files announced, downloads started and finished, file sizes, errors and their system codes. • The camera's model name, as the camera reports it. What neither contains: no serial number, no photo or picture, no catalogue or edits, no location, no name or email address, no Apple Account details. File names are not in the Compatibility Report. In the TestFlight and App Store versions the Camera Log shows a file name as "[file]" with its extension (for example "[file].NEF"); file sizes stay. Please read both before you send them; if you see anything you do not want to share, remove it or do not send it. What we do with them: we use them only to check, add and fix camera support, and to answer you. We do not sell them, use them for advertising or analytics, or pass them to anyone else. We keep them only as long as needed for that, and for at most 12 months, and delete them on request (write to wireshot@icloud.com). We may list which camera models were tested and how they behaved, without any information about you. 4.
